Optional in-car radio
=====================

This SD pack does not include copyrighted music.  The game supports up to
15 two-minute clips in this directory, named 01.wav ... 15.wav (8 kHz,
8-bit, mono PCM WAV).  Pressing enter next to a car loads one clip while
Tommy walks to it; entering starts it at a random offset, and exiting stops
it.  Options -> radio turns in-car playback on or off and is saved.

Without these files everything else works normally; the radio simply stays
silent.
